Minimum roll

How much of a rare's own roll a listing has to match to count as comparable. At 0% any item with the same three good mods matches at any value, and reports the price of the worst one.

Ignored for uniques, except timeless and Time-Lost jewels, where the number is the item.

Which listings count

The trade site's own dropdown. "In Person" sellers have to be messaged and may never reply, so their asking prices are softer than what you would actually pay.

Corrupted uniques

Match the implicit a corruption added. A Le Heup of All with "+1 to Maximum Power Charges" is worth many times a plain one, and pricing them the same throws the whole build's total off.

Turn this off if a corrupted unique finds nothing — the implicit may be rare enough that no copy is listed.

Cache

Cached prices

Trade appraisals are kept for two hours, keyed on the item, the league and the settings above. That is what makes re-opening a build free instead of spending GGG's request budget again.

About

What it does

Prices a poe.ninja character from poe.ninja's public economy API and the official trade site. It never calls poe.ninja's builds API: that one is documented as internal, so the extension reads only what the page has already painted.

The trade pass takes two to six minutes and is paced to stay well inside GGG's rate limit, leaving room for your own searches.

Not official

An independent tool. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or approved by Grinding Gear Games, and not affiliated with or endorsed by poe.ninja either. Neither has reviewed it.

Path of Exile is a trademark of Grinding Gear Games, and the item names, art and game data are theirs. This reads what is already on the page and what two public APIs return, and redistributes none of it.

Prices are an estimate with a floor, worked out from listings other players wrote. Not a valuation, and not a promise that anything sells at that number.

PoE Ninja Build Cost

Minimum roll

Applies to every rare — armour, weapons, jewellery and jewels alike. It sets how much of the item's own roll a listing has to match to count as comparable. At 0% an item with three good modifiers matches anything that has those modifiers at any value, and reports the price of the worst one.

Ignored for uniques, whose modifier ranges are narrow enough that a minimum only costs listings — except timeless and Time-Lost jewels, where the number is the item.

Which listings count

The same choice as the trade site's own dropdown. "In Person" sellers have to be messaged and may never reply, so their asking prices are softer than what you would actually pay.

Cached prices

Trade appraisals are kept for two hours, keyed on the item, the league and the settings above. That is what makes re-opening a build free instead of spending GGG's request budget again.
